Chilli Powder Sourcing Standards

Our Export Sourcing and Sorting Quality Parameters

Our Guntur red chillies are carefully selected based on export grading specifications. We check physical dimensions, stem presence (stemmed or stemless options), skin thickness, and seed content. Each batch is sorted manually by experienced grading workers who discard discolored, damaged, or broken pods. We maintain the moisture percentage strictly between 10% to 12% to prevent mold formation during transit. Importers can customize their orders based on heat values measured in Scoville Heat Units (SHU) ranging from mild Byadgi (10,000 SHU) to highly spicy Teja S17 (80,000+ SHU) depending on regional market applications.

Strict Chemical and Microbial Testing Specs

Quality control is central to our exporting philosophy. Our laboratory tests check for aflatoxin levels (B1, B2, G1, G2), ochratoxin A, and pesticide residues to meet EU, US, and Middle Eastern import regulations. We ensure the physical specifications have a maximum of 2% broken chillies, less than 1% foreign matter, and less than 4% loose seeds. Every batch is certified with phytosanitary certificates, SGS or Geo-Chem reports, and fumigation certificates before shipment to guarantee clean, insect-free spices that pass customs smoothly at the port of destination.

Custom Packing and Shipping Logistics

We provide multiple packing options suited to international transport. Our bulk options include standard 25 kg PP bags, 10 kg jute bags, or custom cartoon boxes. For high-grade chilli powder, we offer barrier bags with vacuum sealing to protect the volatile oils and color index from oxidation. Our strategic logistics team coordinates container stuffing inside climate-controlled warehouses. We handle fast road transport to major Indian export gateways, including Chennai Port, Vishakapatnam Port, and Krishnapatnam Port, managing the entire documentation workflow from Bill of Lading to Certificate of Origin.
